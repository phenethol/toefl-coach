## How responses are scored

Email and Academic Discussion get two scores (0–5):

- The **ETS estimate** is the score on ETS's official rubric, calibrated blind on ETS's rated responses ([rules](../.claude/skills/toefl-writing-grader/references/ets-calibration.md)). Typos are tolerated; task completion, development and errors that get in the way decide it. It comes with **engine flags**: places where an automated scorer might give less than a human.
- The **strict estimate** is harsher: every genuine error, limited or misused vocabulary, generic development and risks for the automated scorer lower it.

Build a Sentence is scored k/10 and has a single score.

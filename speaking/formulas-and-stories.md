# Formulas for each question type + your story bank

The formulas (parts 1–2) work for anyone. The story bank (part 3) is yours: it is filled from your own answers.
- Stories contain **only real details** from your life. The coach never invents them.
- When the coach sees a good real detail in an answer, it will suggest adding it here.

**Phrases and ready-made sentences for the stories:** [word-bank.md](word-bank.md)

---

## 1. The general formula: A → R → S → C

| Part | Timer | What |
|---|---|---|
| **A**nswer | 00:45 → 00:40 | one sentence that answers the question directly, using words from the question |
| **R**eason | 00:40 → 00:30 | *because…*, one clear thought |
| **S**tory | 00:30 → 00:12 | one of the stories below, cut to 2–3 sentences |
| **C**onclusion | **orange** → 00:03 | *So overall…* + the position from the first sentence |

**Bridges into a story:**
- *In my case, for example, …*
- *I've actually experienced this myself: …*
- *A good example from my own life is …*
- *Where I live, in <city>, …*
- *Just last semester, …*

## 2. Formulas by question type (ETS order)

### Q1: experience (*Think about the last time… What did you do? Who…?*)
**Formula:** when and what → what happened in 2–3 steps → one memorable detail → a reflection
> *Last weekend, I…* → *First… then… in the end…* → *The best part was…* → ***So it was exactly what I needed after a busy week.***

- Answer **every** question in the prompt (*what* and *who* and *why*).
- Don't stop at 35 s: the reflection (*why it mattered*) fills the last 5–8 s.

### Q2: preference or reaction + why (*Which describes you better? Why do you think that is?*)
**Formula:** choice → why (a trait, a habit) → a story as evidence → a small exception → conclusion
> *I'm definitely more of a… person.* → *I think that's because…* → *In my case, for example…* → *Of course, sometimes…* → ***So overall, …***

- The first five words are the choice. *It depends* only comes later, as the exception.

### Q3: opinion (*Some people believe… Do you agree? Why or why not?*)
**Formula:** *I agree / I disagree* → main reason → story → concession → conclusion
> ***I disagree.*** *I think…* → *because…* → *I've experienced this myself: …* → *Of course, some people might say…, but…* → ***So overall, …***

- The first word is *agree* or *disagree*, with no warm-up.

### Q4: broader question or policy (*Do you think universities/stores/cities should…? Why or why not?*)
**Formula:** position → a reason for people in general → a story as evidence → what would happen → conclusion
> *Yes, I think they should.* → *because most students/people…* → *I've seen this myself: …* → *If they did this, …* → ***So overall, …***

Variants:
- **comparison** (*who should do more?*): *Both matter, but mainly…*
- **cause + solution:** half the time on *The main reason is…*, half on *As for solutions…*
- **future:** *I expect that… / It's likely that…*, then a hedge, *but probably not…*

### Listen and Repeat
**Formula:** listen in 3 chunks → repeat the rhythm, not single words
> *Please stay on the paths / and keep off / the newly planted grass.*

- Watch the small words next to the verb: ***off, on, up, for***, and ***the/a***. They decide between a 5 and a 3.
- In a long sentence with an inserted part, first remember the frame (*The science building … has labs…*), then put the middle back in.

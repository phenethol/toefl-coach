# Word bank for personal experience

Phrases and ready-made sentences for your stories in [formulas-and-stories.md](formulas-and-stories.md).
- The sentences use only facts from your stories. Parts in **[square brackets]** suggest a feeling or an opinion, so use them only if they are true.
- The goal is not to memorize everything (memorized answers score lower on the real test), but to have 3–4 phrases per story ready.

---

## 1. Universal phrases for any story

**Setting the scene (when)**
- *Ever since I moved to <city>, …*
- *Back in high school, …* / *When I was a kid, …*
- *These days, …* / *For the past few months, …*
- *Just last semester, …* / *The last time I…, …*

**What happened**
- *At first, … but eventually …*
- *It wasn't until … that I realized …*
- *Before I knew it, …*
- *In the end, …* / *Looking back, …*

**How it felt**
- *It was harder than I expected.* / *It took some getting used to.*
- *I got stuck on …* / *I struggled with …* / *I managed to …*
- *It was a real eye-opener.*
- *It paid off.*

**What you learned** (the key bridge to an opinion)
- *It taught me that …*
- *That's when I realized that …*
- *It changed the way I think about …*
- *I've come to appreciate …*

**From experience to opinion** (Q3 and Q4)
- *Speaking from experience, …*
- *Based on my own experience, …*
- *That's exactly why I think …*
- *I've seen this firsthand: …*

**Honesty and hedging**
- *To be honest, …* / *I have to admit …*
- *It's not perfect, but …* / *It's a trade-off.*

**Ending**
- *So overall, …* / *All in all, …* / *At the end of the day, …*

**Grammar of experience** (the most common errors in recordings)

| What you are saying | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a finished event | past simple | *I **moved** to <city> to study.* |
| experience up to now | present perfect | *I**'ve learned** a lot from it.* |
| something still going on | present perfect continuous | *I**'ve been working** on it for months.* |
| an old habit | used to / would | *I **used to** hate PE classes.* |
| a current habit | present simple | *I usually **eat** out.* (not *I'm eating out*) |

## 2. Phrases per story

*Filled in when you add stories to the bank.* For each story: 8–10 phrases and 3 ready-made sentences.

---

## 3. How to practise the word bank
1. Every day, pick **2 stories** and say them out loud, each with **3 phrases** from the list.
2. Then say them **without looking**, in 15 s each.
3. In the next set, use **at least one phrase** from the universal list in every answer (e.g. *Looking back, …* or *Speaking from experience, …*). The coach will notice it and mention it in the feedback.

**Listen and Repeat:** *You are learning to give campus tours to new students. Listen to the tour guide and repeat what she says.*
1. Welcome to the main campus, everyone.
2. The library is straight ahead, past the fountain.
3. You can borrow laptops for free at the library's front desk.
4. The dining hall on your left is open until nine.
5. Please stay on the paths and keep off the newly planted grass.
6. If you have questions about housing, the student center can help you.
7. The science building, which opened last year, has labs that first-year students can use.

**Take an Interview:** *how young adults spend their free time*
1. A typical weekend in the past month: what did you do, and who with?
2. Recharging alone vs with friends: which describes you better, and why?
3. Should hobbies have a practical purpose, e.g. a career skill? Why or why not?
4. Should universities limit homework on weekends? Why or why not?

### Section 2: orientation week + money
Video: [speaking-audio/speaking-section-2](../speaking-audio/speaking-section-2/) ; definition: [sets/speaking-section-2.json](sets/speaking-section-2.json)

<details>
<summary>Text (open only after practising)</summary>

**Listen and Repeat:** *You are training to work at the welcome desk during orientation week. Listen to the coordinator and repeat what she says.*
1. Orientation week starts on Monday morning.
2. You can buy textbooks at the campus bookstore.
3. Buses to the city center leave every fifteen minutes.
4. Make sure you sign up for a gym membership online.
5. The health center is closed on weekends, except for medical emergencies.
6. Please turn off your phones before you enter the main lecture hall.
7. The career office, located next to the library, helps first-year students find part-time jobs.

**Take an Interview:** *how young adults manage their money*
1. The last time you bought something expensive that you had to save up for: what did you buy and why?
2. Careful planner or spontaneous spender: which describes you better, and why?
3. Should high schools have a required personal-finance course? Why or why not?
4. Should stores be allowed to refuse cash? Why or why not?

</details>

**Technology for learning**
1. How do you usually use your phone or computer when you study? Tell me about a typical study session.
2. Some people prefer learning from videos, while others prefer reading books or articles. Which do you prefer, and why?
3. Many schools now allow students to use AI tools for homework. Do you think this helps students learn, or does it make them rely on technology too much?
4. How do you think technology will change the role of teachers over the next twenty years?

**Food and eating habits**
1. What does a normal day of eating look like for you? Do you usually cook for yourself, or do you eat out?
2. Would you rather have a meal at home with family or friends, or go out to a restaurant? Why?
3. Some people believe universities should offer only healthy food in their cafeterias. Do you agree or disagree?
4. Do you think the way people eat will change in the future? For example, will many more people stop eating meat? Why or why not?

**Sport and physical activity**
1. How do you usually stay active? Tell me about the kind of exercise you do, or would like to do.
2. Would you rather exercise on your own or with other people, for example in a team or a group class? Why?
3. Some people think universities should make physical education a required course for all students. Do you agree or disagree?
4. It seems that fewer young people play sports today than in the past. Why do you think this is happening, and what could be done about it?

**Where young people choose to live**
1. Can you describe the place where you live now? What do you like most about it, and what would you change?
2. Would you rather live in a big city or in a small town? Why?
3. Some people believe young adults should move out of their parents' home as soon as they finish school. Do you agree or disagree?
4. In many countries, cities are becoming too expensive for young people. What do you think cities or governments should do about this problem?

**Travel and tourism**
1. Tell me about a trip you took recently. Where did you go, and what was the best part of it?
2. When you travel, do you prefer to plan everything in advance or to be more spontaneous? Why?
3. Some people think tourism does more harm than good to popular destinations. What's your opinion?
4. How do you think the way people travel will change in the next twenty years?

**Students and work**
1. Do you have a job right now, or have you had one in the past? Tell me about it.
2. Would you rather have a part-time job while you study, or focus only on your studies? Why?
3. Some people believe companies should always pay students for internships, even short ones. Do you agree or disagree?
4. Many people think artificial intelligence will replace a lot of jobs in the future. How do you think this will affect young people who are just starting their careers?

**Social media**
1. Which social media apps do you use the most, and what do you usually use them for?
2. Would you rather keep in touch with friends online or meet them in person? Why?
3. Some countries have decided that children under sixteen should not be allowed to use social media. Do you agree with this kind of rule?
4. How do you think social media will change the way people get their news in the future?

</details>

## How answers are scored

Every Interview answer gets two scores (0–5):

- The **ETS estimate** predicts what an ETS rater would give. It was calibrated blind on ETS's own rated responses ([method](../.claude/skills/toefl-coach/references/ets-calibration-report.md)). **Relevance** (every part of the question), **elaboration** (at least one concrete personal example or detail), **coherence** and a **conversational pace** decide it. Fillers, self-corrections, plain vocabulary and minor slips do not stop a 5.
- The **strict estimate** uses stricter criteria as a training target. It takes points off for fillers and hesitation, basic or repeated vocabulary, no single deep concrete example, length outside 90–100 words, no clear ending and running over time.

Listen and Repeat is scored with the ETS guide (0–5 per sentence).
Pronunciation is judged only indirectly in the scores (how reliably Whisper recognizes the words), plus a separate pronunciation and delivery signal that never changes a score.

### Focus (to start with)
1. **Conclusion on orange:** when the timer shows the last 10 s, start *So overall…* and repeat the position from your first sentence.
2. **One concrete example in every answer:** a name, a place or a situation from your own life.
3. **Short, finished sentences.** When you feel a third *and* or *because* coming, put in a full stop.
